FlowPilot Revenue Intelligence OS
Project Overview

FlowPilot Revenue Intelligence OS is a portfolio capstone project designed to demonstrate how AI agents, workflow automation, CRM systems, and business analytics can work together to support go-to-market operations.

Business Problem

Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success teams often work across disconnected tools, creating manual handoffs, inconsistent lead qualification, and limited visibility into the customer lifecycle.

Proposed Solution

Build an integrated system that captures leads, applies qualification logic, updates CRM records, and provides business intelligence.

Development Status

In progress — Week 1: Lead intake and automation foundations.
